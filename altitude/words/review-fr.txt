These are the words that can be the day's word, by length, in alphabetical order.
In the game they come in a fixed shuffled order (climb/words/fr.json, "answers"):
day N takes word number (N mod list length).
To remove or add words, list them and Claude rebuilds the file; the order of the
remaining days stays fixed only if words are added at the end, so changes are best
made before the game opens.
