    How to play

    1. A real food or drink at the top.
    2. Drag up on the plate to add, down to take away. Stop at the same energy.
    3. Tap Show the real amount. Yellow marks the part you got wrong.

    One a day, the same for everyone. A new one arrives at midnight.

    Where the figures come from

    Two official food tables, FoodData Central (USDA) and Matvaretabellen (Norway), both linked under the answer. Rounded to half a portion.

    A portion: apple 180 g, banana 120 g, slice of bread 30 g, egg 50 g, grape 5 g; a glass 250 ml, a can 330 ml.

    Someone to talk to

    If food or eating is hard for you, you are not alone. These help lines are free and open on weekdays: Beat in the United Kingdom (0808 801 0677; in Scotland 0808 801 0432, in Wales 0808 801 0433, in Northern Ireland 0808 801 0434), the National Alliance for Eating Disorders in the United States (+1 866 662 1235), and SOS Disturbi Alimentari in Italy (800 180 969).
