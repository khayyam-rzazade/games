How to play

  1. Press and hold. The figures get counted one by one. A tap adds one.
  2. While you hold, slide right for more and left for fewer.
  3. Let go at your guess. Hold again to add more, or tap Redo.
  4. Tap Lock in to see how it really is.

One question a day, the same for everyone. A new one arrives at midnight.
