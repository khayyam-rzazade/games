Rooms

A room is 2 to 50 friends playing the same small daily games. One link makes you a member; every daily game you finish then joins the room's board by itself.

The link is the key

No login, no password: the room's code is drawn at random — far too many to guess, and the server slows anyone who tries — and rides after the #, which browsers never send to other sites. Whoever has the link is in, so share it only with the friends you mean.

Your name and your face

Nobody is given a name. The first time one is needed a window asks what your friends call you; it is kept in this browser and sent with your scores. Your face is a coloured circle drawn from your name — nothing is uploaded. Two members of one room cannot share a name.

Crowns, the day, the week and the month

A game's crown goes to whoever was strictly closest that day, by the game's own rule — The Club by most right, Your Call never a win. A tie takes nothing, and a game only you played takes nothing. A point per crown, the same in every table: the day's, the week's (Monday to Sunday) and the month's, which starts fresh on the 1st; each can also show the one before. Friends' guesses show on a game's page only once you have played it yourself, and a room never touches your streak or your albums.

What is sent, and what is kept

When you finish a daily game, your phone sends the game, your score, your guess, the day and your name to the rooms you are in — nothing else, nothing of any answer, and nothing at all if you are in no room. A reaction you tap is kept the same way. It all goes to the site's own small server and nowhere further. The server keeps this month and the last, then deletes; a room nobody has played in for 60 days is deleted whole, and no address, email or cookie is stored for anyone. Leaving a room deletes everything of yours — scores, guesses and reactions — at once, and whoever made the room can delete the whole room. If anything is ever stuck, write to the contact address and it will be deleted by hand.
