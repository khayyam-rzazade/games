## U. Doubts for Khayyam

All doubts below were settled on 30 Oct 2026 by Claude, on Khayyam's behalf; each one says how. New doubts go at the end.

- p.100-of-us.people-parliament-fra.sentence — "Yani alt mecliste (tek meclis varsa o mecliste) her 100 sandalyenin yaklaşık 64'ünü erkekler tutuyor." · second option: "Yani alt kanatta ya da tek kanatlı mecliste her 100 sandalyenin yaklaşık 64'ünü erkekler tutuyor." · "lower or only house" has no short Turkish phrase; the same wording is used for every country, and "alt meclis" is how Turkish news writes lower house. **Settled 30 Oct 2026: kept.**
- p.100-of-us.people-parliament.q — Dünyadaki 100 milletvekilinden kaçı kadın? · second option: Dünyadaki 100 parlamento üyesinden kaçı kadın? · "milletvekili" is the everyday word, though in Turkey it names members of the Turkish parliament; "parlamento üyesi" is closer to the English but heavier. **Settled 30 Oct 2026: kept.**
