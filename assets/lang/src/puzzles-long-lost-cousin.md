Long Lost Cousin has 148 puzzles and started on 4 Oct 2026. Day d plays puzzle (d − 1) mod 148 + 1, so after day 148 the stock comes round again; a line here is one field of one puzzle, and the build lays the language's words over the English puzzle on every day that plays it. Everything not listed (numbers, answers, sources, pictures, the door's order) stays as it is. A puzzle no language has yet shows in English on its day, with one quiet line under the game's name.

## How to read and translate this doc

- A string is one line: `` - `key` — the words ``. The key never changes; only the words after the dash. One string per line, never two lines. Lines starting with ↳ are notes for you, never shown.
- The key names the puzzle by its id, not by a day: `p.long-lost-cousin.<id>.<field>`. A puzzle comes back every 148 days, and your line comes back with it, for ever. Never write a day number in a key.
- Write your file as `assets/lang/src/puzzles-long-lost-cousin-<code>.md` (it, tr, es, pt, fr), with the same headings and keys, only your words after the dash. Write only the puzzles marked **to do** for your language; the ones marked **done** are already translated (their lines are in your file, from the earlier batch): do not redo them and do not change them.
- The voice and the level are the site's: "tu" in Italian, "sen" in Turkish, "tú" in Spanish, "você" in Portuguese, "tu" in French; a 12-year-old can read every line; nothing negative; written for the language rather than translated word for word. A fact — a year, a figure, a rate, a price, a name — is never changed, only said in the language. Numbers are written the language's way (decimal comma, the thousands sign) with the same value.
- Keep `<b>`, `<a href>` and `&nbsp;` where the English has them; keep a `{placeholder}` exactly as written.
- Your doubts go at the end of your file, in a last section `## U. Doubts for Khayyam`: one bullet per line you were unsure about — the key without backticks, the line as you wrote it, a second option, a few words on why. The build reads nothing in that section.

## The rules of Long Lost Cousin for the translator

One animal or plant and three others: which is its closest relative? **What is a fact:** the family tree never changes; only the names and the sentence are yours. **What carries the clue:** nothing in the names may hint at the answer: a name that in the language suggests kinship ("cane della prateria" says "dog") is fine where it is the real name, because the English has the same trap; never add a hint of your own. **What is a name:** every animal has three forms, listed once under `p.long-lost-cousin.thing.<key>`: `name` (on the card, capitalised: "Meerkat"), `the` (in a sentence, with the article: "the meerkat"; Italian "il suricato", Turkish "mirket" (no article), Spanish "el suricato"), `of` (the owner form the question needs: "the meerkat's"; Italian "del suricato", Turkish "mirketin", Spanish "del suricato"). `fact` is the sentence under the answer. "T. rex" and other Latin-style names stay.
