# Logicers: the puzzles of Your Call, in English (every puzzle of the stock, keyed on the puzzle; written by claude/logicers-data/scripts/puzzles_doc.py from your-call/puzzles.js)

Your Call has 90 puzzles and started on 3 Oct 2026. Day d plays puzzle (d − 1) mod 90 + 1, so after day 90 the stock comes round again; a line here is one field of one puzzle, and the build lays the language's words over the English puzzle on every day that plays it. Everything not listed (numbers, answers, sources, pictures, the door's order) stays as it is. A puzzle no language has yet shows in English on its day, with one quiet line under the game's name.

## How to read and translate this doc

- A string is one line: `` - `key` — the words ``. The key never changes; only the words after the dash. One string per line, never two lines. Lines starting with ↳ are notes for you, never shown.
- The key names the puzzle by its id, not by a day: `p.your-call.<id>.<field>`. A puzzle comes back every 90 days, and your line comes back with it, for ever. Never write a day number in a key.
- Write your file as `assets/lang/src/puzzles-your-call-<code>.md` (it, tr, es, pt, fr), with the same headings and keys, only your words after the dash. Write only the puzzles marked **to do** for your language; the ones marked **done** are already translated (their lines are in your file, from the earlier batch): do not redo them and do not change them.
- The voice and the level are the site's: "tu" in Italian, "sen" in Turkish, "tú" in Spanish, "você" in Portuguese, "tu" in French; a 12-year-old can read every line; nothing negative; written for the language rather than translated word for word. A fact — a year, a figure, a rate, a price, a name — is never changed, only said in the language. Numbers are written the language's way (decimal comma, the thousands sign) with the same value.
- Keep `<b>`, `<a href>` and `&nbsp;` where the English has them; keep a `{placeholder}` exactly as written.
- Your doubts go at the end of your file, in a last section `## U. Doubts for Khayyam`: one bullet per line you were unsure about — the key without backticks, the line as you wrote it, a second option, a few words on why. The build reads nothing in that section.

## The rules of Your Call for the translator

One real moment from history, three choices, then what the real leader did. **What is a fact:** the year, the place, the leader's name, what was done and what followed: never changed, only said in the language. **What carries the clue:** the three choices must give nothing away: keep each one about as long and as plain as the others, and keep their order (choice 1, 2, 3; the real one is not marked here). **What is a name:** `short` is the leader's short name as the language writes it ("Kennedy" stays; "Prince Carl" may become "il principe Carl"). `place` is a city or country in the language ("Copenaghen"). `year` appears only for a year written with BC ("480 BC"): say it the language's way ("480 a.C.", "MÖ 480", "480 av. J.-C."), the same as in Years Apart, and nothing else. `pov` is the moment written to the player as "you"; the page adds "POV:" before and "Your call." after it. `did` and `next` are the two paragraphs of the answer. `stamp` is the album stamp (3–6 words). The sources stay.
