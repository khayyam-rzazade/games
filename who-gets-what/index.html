      How to play

      1. Something real and its price, in one country, in one year.
      2. The bar is that whole price. Drag the lines until the split looks right to you.
      3. Tap Show the split, and see how much you put in the wrong place.

      One price a day, the same for everyone. A new one arrives at midnight.

      Where the splits come from

      Published by bodies that measure them, checked against a second source; both linked under the answer. A slice is often a whole step of the chain: "Shop" can mean rent and staff as well as profit.
