Ogni domanda di Scopri chi sei viene da una scala di ricerca pubblicata ed è mostrata parola per parola, con la sua scala di risposta pubblicata. Quello che è nostro (i nomi, le istruzioni di una riga, qualche frase di riempimento, i nomi dei Paesi) è segnalato. Ogni piccolo cambiamento fatto per il telefono è nella tabella delle deviazioni. I punteggi si calcolano con le regole qui sotto, lo stesso codice sul telefono e sul server. Le risposte sono conservate solo per chi dice di sì alla fine, senza nome, email o indirizzo; i dati anonimi saranno pubblicati dopo il primo articolo, resi meno precisi perché nessuno possa essere riconosciuto. Find out about yourself — item sheet, Italian (version 1, 23 October 2026; item_version 10)

The Italian companion to claude/find-outs-items.md (English v1.1): the same nine games, the same scoring, the same bands (their Italian names come from the copy doc, left blank here), the same conventions on screen (one item per screen, Skip, Back, the readout "4 di 5"). Only the words change. Everything below the English sheet's rules is unchanged unless this file says otherwise.

How each text was chosen (the handoff's order): 1. an official or authors' Italian version; 2. a validated Italian version printed in a peer-reviewed article; 3. our translation, back-translated into English beside it, marked in the Deviations table as "our translation, back-translated, not validated". Every Italian source below was opened on 23 October 2026. All reading went through a fetch tool that extracts the text of a page or PDF (the shell has no internet); the ESS PDFs were also opened in a browser and their text layer read directly. Where a quote came only through the fetch tool's extraction, this is said. Nothing is quoted from memory.

Register. The Italian survey sources address the respondent formally ("Lei"), as every Italian questionnaire does. Their items are kept verbatim, so our own item translations use "Lei" too, and so do the instructions under "?". The on-screen lines around the items are ours and live in the copy doc (which may use "tu"); a mix of "tu" around the items and "Lei" inside them is normal in Italian web surveys. Gender. Italian adjectives agree with the person. Where the source writes both forms with a slash ("soddisfatta/o", "un cittadino svizzero / una cittadina svizzera") we keep the slash, as published. Where a source uses the masculine alone (ESS "interessato") we keep it, as published. Our own translations use the slash only where Italian leaves no neutral way (D37).

Game 1 — "Your personality" · Big Five personality · BFI-10

Source (opened). Guido, G., Peluso, A. M., Capestro, M., & Miglietta, M. (2015). An Italian version of the 10-item Big Five Inventory: An application to hedonic and utilitarian shopping values. Personality and Individual Differences, 76, 135–140. doi:10.1016/j.paid.2014.11.053. Read from the full-text copy on academia.edu (item 25313501): Method (professional forward–backward translation), Table 1 and Appendix A ("Italian version of the BFI-10 scale"). The publisher's page and the IRIS Pavia record (no file) could not be opened.

Published stem (Appendix A, verbatim): Mi vedo come una persona che… Instruction under "?" (ours — the Italian paper prints none): Quanto bene descrivono la Sua personalità le seguenti affermazioni? Back-translation: "How well do the following statements describe your personality?" (D25)

# Item (verbatim, Appendix A) Trait Keyed
1 … è riservata Extraversion R
2 … generalmente si fida Agreeableness +
3 … tende a essere pigra Conscientiousness R
4 … è rilassata, sopporta bene lo stress Neuroticism R
5 … ha pochi interessi artistici Openness R
6 … è spigliata, socievole Extraversion +
7 … tende a trovare i difetti negli altri Agreeableness R
8 … è coscienziosa nel lavoro Conscientiousness +
9 … si agita facilmente Neuroticism +
10 … ha una fervida immaginazione Openness +

Appendix A keeps Rammstedt & John's order, so the numbers match the English sheet. The adjectives agree with "una persona", so the items read the same for women and men.

A difference inside the paper. Table 1 prints item 8 as "è coscienziosa al lavoro" and item 4 without "bene" ("è rilassata, sopporta lo stress"). Appendix A — the instrument as administered — has "nel lavoro" and "sopporta bene lo stress". We use Appendix A. Worth one look at the publisher's PDF with Genoa access.

Response format (verbatim ends, Appendix A): 1 Per niente d'accordo … 5 Del tutto d'accordo. Points 2–4 are unlabelled in the Italian version. On screen they carry our words (D26): 2 In parte in disaccordo · 3 Né d'accordo né in disaccordo · 4 In parte d'accordo Back-translation: "Disagree in part · Neither agree nor disagree · Agree in part". Scoring (Appendix A's key, the same as the English): Guido et al. name the fourth trait emotional stability (item 4 +, item 9 R); that is the reverse of Neuroticism, i.e. our "Calm" bar. Reverse 1, 3, 4 (for N), 5, 7; each trait the mean of its two items. Nothing changes.

# Item (ours) Back-translation
1 … nel mondo accadano molte cose molto importanti di cui il pubblico non viene mai informato. … many very important things happen in the world which the public is never informed about.
2 … di solito i politici non ci dicano i veri motivi delle loro decisioni. … politicians usually do not tell us the true motives for their decisions.

Game 6 — "Four riddles" · Cognitive reflection · CRT-2

No Italian CRT-2 exists (none found). Ours, back-translated (D32). The structure, the lure, the answers and the four-option rule stay as in English.

# Item (ours) Correct Lure Two other wrong options Back-translation
1 Se durante una gara superi la persona al secondo posto, in che posizione sei? Secondo Primo Terzo · Ultimo If during a race you pass the person in second place, what position are you in?
2 Un contadino aveva 15 pecore e sono morte tutte tranne 8. Quante ne restano? 8 7 15 · 0 A farmer had 15 sheep and all died except 8. How many are left?
3 Il padre di Sofia ha tre figlie. Le prime due si chiamano Aprile e Maggio. Come si chiama la terza figlia? Sofia Giugno Luglio · Marzo Sofia's father has three daughters. The first two are called Aprile and Maggio. What is the third daughter called?
4 Quanti metri cubi di terra ci sono in una buca profonda 3 m, larga 3 m e lunga 3 m? Nessuno 27 9 · 3 How many cubic metres of earth are there in a hole 3 m deep, 3 m wide and 3 m long?

The riddles use "tu", as riddles do in Italian; "Lei" would sound like a form. Khayyam decides (D37).

The names in riddle 3. The trap works because the first two names are months, so the mind runs on to the next month. In Italian the months are Aprile, Maggio, Giugno. "Aprile" is a real, rare girl's name; "Maggio" is not a name at all — but in the English riddle "April" and "May" are also chosen for the months, and the Italian reader sees the series at once, which is what makes "Giugno" pull. The correct answer had to be a common girl's name that is not a month: Sofia (any common name works). "Giulia" was avoided because it comes from the same root as "Luglio" (July), one of the wrong options. The two other wrong options are the next and the previous month (Luglio, Marzo), as in English (July, March). Second option for Khayyam: "Le prime due si chiamano Aprile e Maia" (Maia is a real name and close to "Maggio") — closer to real names, weaker trap. We recommend the months. Riddle 4: metres, as in English (D15); "buca" is the everyday word for a hole dug in the ground. After the four (ours): Avevi già visto qualcuno di questi indovinelli? Sì · No ("Had you seen any of these riddles before? Yes · No"). Explanations on the reveal: they are copy, not items; the Italian copy doc has them.
