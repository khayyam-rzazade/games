    Same Street photos: Dollar Street, a Gapminder project. Free material from GAPMINDER.ORG, CC-BY LICENSE. The photographer is named under each answer. Dollar Street does not endorse this site.

    Animal silhouettes: PhyloPic. Artists who ask for credit are named under each answer. PhyloPic does not endorse this site.

    Same Energy: the US FoodData Central and Norway's Matvaretabellen. Twin or Trap: two dictionaries, named under each answer. So-Called Expert: one fact is invented on purpose, and marked as invented.

    Who Was Closer

    Send a link from any daily game. A friend plays the same day and you both see who was closer. The scores travel inside the link, after the #, which browsers never send anywhere. Your chosen name is kept in this browser and goes inside the links. See what your friend sees.

    Rooms

    A room is 2 to 50 friends with a shared table. When a member finishes a daily game, their phone sends the game, score, guess, day and chosen name to our small server. Nothing at all for anyone in no room. We keep this month and the last. A room nobody plays in for 60 days is deleted. Leaving deletes everything of yours, and the room's maker can delete the room. Make or join a room.

    The world's counts

    "The world today" compares you with your continent and country, and the World table ranks countries. Each finished daily game sends the game, how far off you were and, for one-number games, your guess to our small server. It reads your country from your connection and keeps only counts: no address, nothing that identifies you, a guess only as an average of two or more. Counts are deleted after 75 days. You can leave your results out under the ? on the World table page.

    What players said

    When your day's games are done, you can write one line about what surprised you. Khayyam reads it before it shows, and it shows with your country's flag. You can also add a name and an “I am a…” (a student, a teacher…); both are optional, and they show with your line only if you typed them and he lets it through. Write only what you want others to see. A line he lets through is kept a year; the others are deleted within a month. The line, what you typed with it, your country and the date are all that is kept. To have yours removed, write to logicers.world@gmail.com.

    Live games

    In How Sure?, a host opens a game and everyone joins with a code. No account. Names and answers stay on our small server until a day after the game ends (backups clear within 30 days). Anyone can build their own game; Logicers does not check them, and every screen has a Report link. A saved game keeps what its maker typed, plus players' names and answers when played alone, and is deleted two months after its last use. An email left to recover a link is kept only as a one-way fingerprint, and the address itself only if feedback emails are ticked. Play live · Build your own.
