        How the table works

        1. Every daily game you finish scores from 0 to 100: spot on is 100, and the further off, the fewer.
        2. A country's score is the average of its players' games that day, made fair across games, so nobody is ahead for playing the easier ones.
        3. A country with only a few games is pulled toward the world's average until more of its players join. A handful of lucky games can't top the table; every friend who plays makes your country's own score count for more.
        4. A country takes a place from 3 games in a day, or 10 in a month.

        Your Call has no right answer, so it shows how the world chose and counts in the games played, but not in the scores. Practice never counts. The day is your own calendar day; the month is the month of today, up to today.
