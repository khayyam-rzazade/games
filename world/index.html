        How the table works

        1. Every daily game you finish scores from 0 to 100: spot on is 100, and the further off, the fewer.
        2. A country's score is the average of its players' games that day, made fair across games, so nobody is ahead for playing the easier ones.
        3. A country above the world's average is pulled back toward it until it has plenty of games, so a handful of lucky games, or one person playing again and again, can't top the table.
        4. A country is a team: every game its players finish adds to a team bonus of up to 25 points. It grows fast at first and is full at 300 games in a day (1,500 in a week, 6,000 in a month). Every friend who plays helps, but past that only playing well counts.
        5. A country takes a place from 10 games in a day, 20 in a week, or 30 in a month.

        Your Call has no right answer, so it shows how the world chose; it counts in the games played and the bonus, never in the average. Practice never counts. The day is your own calendar day; the week runs from Monday and the month from its first day, both up to today.
