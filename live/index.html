Your privacy

No login and no cookies. Live games: the name you type and your answers stay on Logicers' server for the game and are deleted a day after it ends. Games people make: a saved game keeps only what its maker typed into it, with no name of the maker, and an email only as a one-way fingerprint, if the maker leaves one as a way back to a lost link (the address itself only if they also tick feedback emails, and only for those); it is deleted two months after its last use. Playing one alone keeps the name you type and your answers with the game, for its scoreboard, and they go when the game goes. A report keeps what its sender writes, the game's code, and a name and email only if given, for six months. The server's own backups clear within 30 days. Nothing else about you is kept.

Games people make

Anyone can build a game with no account: Build your own. Logicers does not check games people make; every screen of one has a Report link. The terms say what is not allowed and how reports are handled.
