Lost edit link

How it works

When saving, a maker can leave an email as a way back. Logicers keeps only a one-way fingerprint of it, so type the address in the From line of their message, exactly: not one written in the message, and not a Reply-To. Only the same address finds their games (capitals make no difference; for Gmail, dots and anything after a + make none either, and the + part none for Outlook, iCloud, Proton and Fastmail; elsewhere they count). "Make a new edit link" replaces the game's key at once: the old link stops working. Send the new one with "Email the new link" here, which writes to the address you typed; never with Reply in your mail app, since a message can send its replies somewhere else. Then a forged From line gets nothing: the link goes to the real address. When the email was set long after the game was made, its card says so. This page sends only the fingerprint, never the address.

Feedback emails

How it works

Makers who ticked "Email me for feedback" next to the email of their way back. Only they are here, and only for this: write to them with your own mail app, in their language, with every address in Bcc, never in To or Cc, so nobody sees another's. Anyone can type any address, so a tick is a request, not a proof: say in each email why it came and how to stop (a reply asking it is enough). "Remove" here takes the address off at once and keeps it off: a tick with it is refused from then on. An address also goes by itself when its owner taps Stop in a game they ticked it in, or when those games are deleted or get another email.

Players said

How it works

At the end of a day's nine games a player may write one line: "What surprised you today?". Nothing shows until you let it through here. A line you let through shows on the finish card of that day and on the home page the next day, with its country's flag and no name, and is kept a year. A line you turn down is deleted after 7 days; one you never look at, after 30. Let through what is honest and harmless; turn down anything about a person, anything rude, and anything that is not a line about the games.
