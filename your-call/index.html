How to play

  1. Read the moment. It really happened.
  2. Tap the choice you would make, then tap "See what they did".
  3. See what the real leader did, and what happened next.

There is no right or wrong choice here. The game only shows what was actually done.

One moment a day, the same for everyone. A new one arrives at midnight.

Every moment is checked against two sources, linked under the answer. Events are at least twenty years old.
