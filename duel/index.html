What your friend sees at the end of the day

How it works

  1. Play any of today's games. They take about a minute each.
  2. At the end, tap Send my day. Your phone makes one link and you send it yourself.
  3. Your friend plays the same day. After each game, you both see who was closer.

Who Was Closer

One link carries your scores for one day in the daily games. A friend who opens it plays the same day and, at the end of each game, sees who was closer. At the end there is a card you can send on.

No account, and no server

There is nothing to join and nothing to install. The scores travel inside the link itself, in the part after the #, which browsers never send to any server — not to ours either. What your phone has merged is kept only in this browser.

Your name

Nobody is given a name here. The first time you send a day, a window asks what your friends call you — that is the only thing this site ever asks you to type, and it asks once. The name is kept in this browser only, and it travels inside every link you send, so pick one your friends will know you by. You can change it at the end of any game.

One day at a time

A duel belongs to its own day. A link from an earlier day says so, and a friend in a later time zone can send you a day that opens at your own midnight. Your streak and your album are never touched by any of this.

In a group chat

Everyone sends their own link, and every phone adds up the links it has opened. Up to eight players at one table. A link only ever carries the day, the scores and the name the sender chose for themselves.

Your privacy

No login, no tracking, no cookies. A duel link carries only the day, the scores and the name the sender chose, inside the link itself. What your phone has merged is kept only in this browser.
