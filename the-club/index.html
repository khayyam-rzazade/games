      How to play

      1. Look at who is in the club and who is not. One secret rule decides it.
      2. Five more come to the door, one at a time. Tap In or Out. You see at once whether you were right, so every answer tells you more about the rule.
      3. After the fifth, the sign of the club lights up with the rule.

      One club a day, the same for everyone. A new one arrives at midnight.

      The rule

      The rule is always a plain fact about the real world. It is never about how a name is spelt.
