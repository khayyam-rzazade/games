SO-CALLED EXPERT: HOW THE GAME IS MADE (6 Oct 2026, night)
=========================================================

What it is
  The third game for groups ("Pass the phone" on the home page), for one phone and three to eight players. In
  group-games.md it is 1.5, "Expert Interview"; that name is taken (search.txt), so it is called So-Called Expert.
  Each player is the expert once. The expert alone sees a card: a country and five facts about it, four true and
  one that Logicers invented, marked with a stamp. The expert presents all five as true, in their own words, and
  takes up to three questions. Then the phone goes to the middle, the five facts show without any mark, and on
  three everyone else points at the one they think is invented. The phone reveals it, always marked "Invented by
  Logicers", with the truth and its sources; then the four true facts with theirs. A point for each player who
  found it; a point for the expert for each player fooled. When everyone has been the expert, most points wins.
  Not daily: no day number, streak or album.

The flow of a turn (the views in so-called-expert.js)
  start      what to do in ten seconds, players (3 to 8, four to begin with), names (optional), Start
  hand       "Turn 2 of 4. Pass the phone to Bo." Everyone else looks away. A face-down card, the points so far.
  card       only the expert: the country, the five facts in the dealt order, the stamp "Invented" on one of them
             ("Say it like the others."), "Done: show the table". It leaves the page when it is put away.
  vote       the same five facts in the same order, all alike; "On three, everyone but Bo points at the number they
             think is invented."; Reveal
  reveal     the invented fact, the stamp "Invented by Logicers", "The truth: ...", its sources; "Who found it?",
             one button per player (pressed or not); "Score it" (or "Nobody found it", "Everyone found it")
  sum        "Bo fooled 2 of 3 · Kenya", the four true facts with their numbers and sources, the points;
             "Pass to Cy" (or "Final scores")
  end        the winner (two who tie share the win; three or more: "3 players share the win!", starred in the list;
             all: "Everyone shares the win!"), every player's points and how many they fooled as the expert, the
             wins at this table; Play again, Share, New players

Decisions (C), by Khayyam's five rules where he did not decide
  - 3 to 8 players: with two, one player alone would guess every turn, and nobody could be fooled "more"; eight, as in
    Still In.
  - One lap: everyone is the expert once, so everyone gets the same chances; a game takes about 2 to 3 minutes a turn.
  - Scoring as group-games.md 1.5 proposed: +1 for each finder, +1 for the expert per player fooled.
  - The five facts are shuffled at every deal, so that the invented one stands in each place as often (checked).
  - No timer and no question counter: the table keeps to "up to three questions" itself; a phone that buzzes would
    break the act. No way back from the vote to the card: the stamp must never show while the phone lies in the middle.
  - Since 6 Oct 2026 every opening of the page starts afresh (below), so the card can never come back by itself.
  - The invented fact is as long and as detailed as the true ones (on 6 Oct the first drafts were the shortest of
    their card on 31 of 46 cards, a tell a table would learn; rewritten, now each place by length about as often:
    t_expert.py D6). Some invented facts use the same words as true facts from the data ("one of only 11 UN members
    whose land the equator crosses"), so that a wording gives nothing away either.
  - The share picture and its message show the country and how many the best bluff fooled, never a fact: an
    invented fact must not travel without its label. Its link deals the same country to a friend's table (as the
    first turn), with the numbers in the friend's line.
  - Neutral facts only: no religion, ethnicity, politics or living people (build.py searches a list of words; the
    two uses of "government" say where a government sits, and are allowed by name).

Where the facts come from (the stock)
  data/so-called-expert.js, written by build.py from cards.py and evidence.txt. 46 cards on 6 Oct 2026: 149 true
  facts checked on two websites (each page opened and read, the quotes in evidence.txt), 35 from the data (the
  checked lists of One of 193, its UN regions, and World Bank figures as in data/one-of-193.js and data/still-in.js),
  46 invented facts, each truth with two or more sources. build.py refuses to write if a rule is broken (its own
  docstring lists them; test/test_build.py breaks each rule once and expects the refusal). cards.csv is the stock as
  a table, written by build.py too.
  The countries: every continent, well-known and less known (Kenya, Ecuador, Peru, Bolivia, Chile, Brazil,
  Argentina, Mexico, Suriname, Canada, Iceland, Finland, Norway, the Netherlands, Italy, Spain, France, Portugal,
  Monaco, Liechtenstein, Türkiye, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Mongolia, Nepal, India, China, Japan, the Philippines,
  Indonesia, New Zealand, Samoa, Papua New Guinea, Egypt, Ethiopia, South Africa, Lesotho, Ghana, DR Congo,
  Madagascar, Colombia, Morocco, Vietnam, Greece, Singapore, Bhutan).
  A table gets the cards it has not had lately first (the last 40 are kept as "recent").

What is kept in the browser (TO.groupUpdate, under groups > so-called-expert)
  games (how many finished) and recent (the card ids of the last 40 turns). The privacy line is the frame's "names"
  one: "The players' names and wins are forgotten when you leave this page."
  Since 6 Oct 2026 (Khayyam: the names got stuck; the game must restart when you go to the home page and come back,
  the names every time the game is opened, and a Restart button) nothing about the players is stored: the setup
  (the number of players and their names), the wins at this table (by name), who is the first expert of the next game
  and the game going on (names, first expert, turn, cards, the order of the facts of each turn, who found it in each
  turn scored, the view) live only while the page is open. Every opening of the page (from the home page, the Back
  button, a reload, or a page the browser brings back from its memory) starts afresh with 4 players and no names;
  what earlier versions stored about the players (n, names, wins, next, cur) is deleted when the page opens. The round
  button ↻ in the bar (only while a game is in play) asks "Restart the game?" and then starts everything over the
  same way; so does "New players" at the end. "Play again" keeps the players and their wins. The window of the scores
  no longer has "End this game" (Restart does it). A pull on the page does not reload it (overscroll-behavior).

Counter events (GoatCounter, without identifying anyone)
  so-called-expert/started, /finished, /share, /challenge-opened. None names a country or a player.
