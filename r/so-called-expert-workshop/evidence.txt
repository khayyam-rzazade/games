SO-CALLED EXPERT: EVIDENCE
==========================
What each page said, for every card of So-Called Expert. Read on 6 October 2026 in the chat that built the game.

How it was done
  - 46 cards were drafted first (r/so-called-expert-workshop/cards.py holds the final words). Each has four true
    facts and one fact that Logicers invented. A true fact either comes from the data files of One of 193 and
    Still In (its checked lists, or the World Bank's open data; build.py checks those against the files) or had to
    be confirmed on two different websites. The invented fact had to be shown false by a source that states the
    truth, which the reveal shows.
  - Six helpers read the pages, each with a fixed number of web searches: round 1, four helpers with 31 each
    (H1 used 28, H2 31, H3 31, H4 28, counting searches the proxy refused); round 2, R1 with 9 (second sources
    where round 1 had only one good one) and R2 with 22 (six new cards: Colombia, Morocco, Vietnam, Greece,
    Singapore, Bhutan). 149 searches in all.
  - Rules the helpers kept (the same words went to each): open only addresses from their own search results or
    links copied from a page they had opened, never an address typed from memory; one of the two sites may be
    English Wikipedia, the other a different site of good standing (no quiz sites, forums, social media, shops,
    AI-written or copied pages); ask the page reader for the sentences word for word and mark any cut with [...];
    report what the page says, also where it differs from the draft.
  - Claude then read every report, changed the words where the sources said something narrower or different
    (part 1), and chose for each fact the two sources shown in the game. build.py checks that each chosen source
    is in part 2 with its quote and address.
  - On 6 Oct 2026 Claude opened one address again to be sure it works for players: the Merriam-Webster entry
    "marmalade" at the address the search gave (prod-ccroc-direct.merriam-webster.com); it showed the entry and the
    same etymology as quoted in H2, PRT-c.

Parts
  1  Changes of wording after the checks, and the sources that are weaker than the rest
  2  The six reports, as the helpers wrote them (#### REPORT H1 #### and so on). Everything in quotation marks in
     part 2 is the pages' words; notes, verdicts, translations and marks are the helpers'.
